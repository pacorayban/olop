Olop

Olop is a personal assistant tool used privately by its owner. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

It uses the open-source command-line tool gogcli, running on the owner's own computer, to let their AI assistant read and manage their own Google accounts (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, Tasks).

Only the owner's own Google accounts are connected.
