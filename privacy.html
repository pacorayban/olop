Olop: privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who uses Olop

Olop is a private tool used only by its owner, with their own Google accounts. No other user can sign in to it.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, Olop accesses data from their own Google accounts: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Contacts and Google Tasks.

How the data is used

The data is used only to let the owner's AI assistant read, organise and act on their own mail, calendar, files, contacts and tasks, at their request. When the owner asks their AI assistant a question, the relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider they use (Anthropic) to produce the answer, under that provider's terms.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own computer, in the operating system keyring. Olop has no server and no database: no Google data is collected, stored or kept by any service run by Olop.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties other than as described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Olop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
